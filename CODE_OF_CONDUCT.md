# Code of conduct

Treat people with respect, review technical work on its merits, and give actionable feedback. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and disclosure of private information are unacceptable in issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.

Report concerns privately to the [repository owner](https://github.com/manikantbindass). Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation. Share only the details needed to review a report.
